# Stronkie's Hunters v1.10

**Final release.** v1.10 is the finished game. It will only change if bugs are found.

New pause menu (press P during play):

- Panel with score, best score, lives, level, ghosts caught and the current place.
- Blinking "** PAUSED **" title.
- P resumes, M switches the music on or off, Q quits to the title (asks Y or N).
- Controls reminder (A/D or stick, space or fire, up or T for the ghost trap).
- The game screen is restored exactly when you resume.
- Everything else is the same as v1.9.

Built and regression-tested in a custom 6502 emulator. Tested on a real Commodore 64 and working.
