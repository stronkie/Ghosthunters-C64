# Stronkie's Zappers v1.12

New hidden bonus: the **S** power-up.

- When you catch a ghost there is a small chance (about 1 in 100) that a falling **S** coin drops, next to the I, E, G and star coins. It never drops in demo mode.
- Catching the S starts a **space raid** in the same part of the city: five ghosts march across the sky, drop one line at every edge of the screen and turn round, and speed up as there are fewer of them.
- Shoot them with the proton beam: every ghost explodes and gives **1000 points**.
- If they reach the street they cost a life and the row starts again at the top.
- When all five are gone the boss of the level appears and the game goes on as normal.

Everything else is the same as v1.11. Built and regression-tested in a custom 6502 emulator. Not yet confirmed on a real C64.
